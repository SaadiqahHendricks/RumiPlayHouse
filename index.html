About Us

A warm second home for curious little minds

Rumi Playhouse offers an intimate early childhood programme in Athlone, Cape Town, serving children ages 2 to 5. Our spaces are warm, earthy and homely — a safe and loving environment for every child.

Inspired by the Reggio Emilia approach, we nurture curiosity through open-ended play, using unconventional materials indoors, outdoors, and in our Atelier — our dedicated Art and Sensory Room.

We value connection, wonder, and the gift of time, allowing children to become genuinely invested in meaningful interactions with materials, peers, and teachers.

Our Vision

We develop the whole child — building self-esteem, security, and confidence for a joyful and successful life. We strive to make joy, wonder, and connection present in everything we do.

Our Approach

Learning through play and wonder

Reggio Emilia

Children are active constructors of knowledge. We provide rich, open-ended materials and a dedicated Atelier where sensory exploration and creativity flourish freely.

Montessori Elements

Child-led pacing and carefully prepared environments that build independence, deep concentration, and a genuine love of learning at every child's own pace.

Holistic Care

We nurture the whole child — social, emotional, physical, and creative. Minimal transitions give children time to dive deep into what truly matters to them.
